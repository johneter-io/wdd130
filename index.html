Haiti

Haiti’s true wealth lies in the exceptional blend of its unique historical heritage
and its boundless creativity. As the first independent black republic,
it possesses a symbolic capital of freedom that infuses its arts,
its world-renowned literature, and its cultural ingenuity.
Beyond its precious natural resources, such as vetiver and its unique landscapes,
it is the resilience of its human capital and the boldness of its youth that constitute its most
promising engine of development for the future.
