THAT'S US

Four decades of mastering Peru's rivers. At The Golden Rafting, adrenaline meets expertise. Our guides, among the most qualified in the country, take you into the heart of the wildest rivers using high-performance equipment.
Don't just choose a tour; choose 40 years of expertise for a unique immersion in the Peruvian wilderness.

Our Mission (Purpose)

At The Golden Rafting, our mission is to turn every rafting trip into an unforgettable human adventure. We believe that the power of the river is the best way to reconnect with nature and strengthen team bonds.

Our Services (Offerings)

We offer a full range of expeditions suited to all skill levels, from gentle currents for families to Class V rapids for experts seeking thrills. Every booking includes state-of-the-art safety equipment, transportation to launch sites, and guidance from passionate, certified guides.
